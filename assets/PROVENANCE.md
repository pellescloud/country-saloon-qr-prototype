# Asset provenance

- servings/*.png: 15 standalone serving illustrations generated with the built-in OpenAI image_gen tool on 22 October 2026. Full neutral-background cup/food plates were generated separately, then downsampled to 256px thumbnails for the web. They are not crops of UI comps or verified photographs of Mack products. Exact prompts are embedded in every PNG and listed in servings/provenance.json. Equal thumbnail dimensions are used for alcohol, alcohol-free items and food.

- logo.png: supplied original Tromsø Countryfestival logo, reused unchanged visually.
- design-a.png, design-b.png, design-c.png: AI-generated design references made with image_gen, with original identity as reference. Exact prompts in matching JSON files and PNG metadata. Not approved final screen specifications.
- design-a-v2.png, design-b-v2.png, design-c-v2.png: updated 22 October 2026 using built-in image_gen edits of the corresponding original comps. Separate beer, cider, seltzer and three wine categories; plastic-cup serving illustrations. Mack product names checked against https://mack.no/produkter/. Wine supplier unconfirmed. Exact generation and correction prompts in matching v2 JSON and PNG metadata. These are illustrative assets, not real product photographs or approved production screens.
- Rye.ttf and Poppins files: existing local font assets; official upstream SIL Open Font License notices accompany the files.
